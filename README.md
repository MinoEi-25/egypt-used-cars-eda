# Egypt Used Cars - Exploratory Data Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of the Egyptian used car market using a dataset of ~600 cars.
The goal is to uncover pricing patterns and market trends.

## Dataset
- 600+ used car listings from the Egyptian market
- Features: Model, Year, Mileage, Color, Transmission, Fuel Type, Price

## Key Questions
- What are the most common cars in Egypt?
- How does price change with manufacturing year?
- Does mileage significantly affect price?
- Which transmission type is more expensive?
- What fuel types dominate the market?

## Tools Used
- Python, Pandas, Matplotlib

## Key Findings
- Kia Cerato is the most common car in the Egyptian used car market
- Car prices generally increase with newer models
- Mileage alone is not a strong price predictor — model and year matter more
- Automatic cars are priced higher than manual
- Gasoline dominates the market by a large margin
